Protect your business before an attacker finds the gap.

What we offer

Cybersecurity services

Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing

Manual and tool-assisted testing of web apps, mobile apps, APIs and internal/external networks, with clear fix guidance.

Security Audits

Review of configurations, access controls, policies and practices against recognised security baselines.

Firewall & Endpoint Protection

Selection, setup and tuning of firewalls, antivirus/EDR and secure configurations for every device.

Incident Response

Rapid containment, investigation and recovery when you suspect a hack, malware or ransomware attack.

Compliance Readiness

Help preparing for data-protection and industry security requirements, including DPDP Act readiness.

Security Awareness Training

Practical sessions so your staff can spot phishing, fraud calls and social-engineering attempts.

Our approach

Security that fits how your business actually runs

Most breaches don't start with sophisticated hacking — they start with an unpatched server, a reused password or an employee clicking the wrong link.

We start by understanding your setup, then focus on the risks most likely to hurt you. You get a prioritised, plain-language report and hands-on help fixing what we find.
