// Digital Forensics

Find out exactly what happened — with evidence that holds up.

When there's fraud, data theft, harassment or a dispute, we examine the digital evidence properly — preserved, analysed and reported so it can be relied upon.

What we offer

Digital Forensics services

Computer Forensics

Forensic imaging and analysis of laptops, desktops and storage to recover activity, files and deleted data.

Mobile Forensics

Extraction and analysis of call logs, chats, media, location history and app data from smartphones.

Email & Social Media Analysis

Tracing email headers, account access, fake profiles and message trails across platforms.

CCTV & Video Forensics

Recovery of footage from DVR/NVR systems, enhancement and authenticity analysis of video.

Fraud & Insider Investigation

Investigation of financial fraud, employee misconduct, IP theft and data leakage.

Expert Reporting

Clear, structured forensic reports suitable for management, legal counsel and authorities.

Our approach

Investigation done right, from the first minute

How evidence is handled in the first few hours decides whether it can be trusted later. Switching on a device, copying files or 'just taking a look' can alter crucial data.

We follow forensically sound procedures — write-blocked acquisition, hash verification and a documented chain of custody — so your findings are defensible.
