// Data Recovery

Lost your data? Don't give up on it yet.

From a single deleted folder to a failed server array, we recover data from damaged, dead and corrupted storage — safely, and without making things worse.

What we offer

Data Recovery services

Hard Disk Recovery

Logical and physical recovery from clicking, not-detected, dropped or water-damaged hard drives.

SSD & NVMe Recovery

Recovery from failed, locked or firmware-corrupted solid-state drives.

RAID, NAS & Server

Reconstruction of failed RAID arrays and recovery from NAS boxes and business servers.

Pen Drive & Memory Cards

Recovery from USB drives, SD/microSD cards and camera media, including formatted or unreadable ones.

Mobile Phone Recovery

Recovery of photos, contacts and chats from damaged or non-booting phones, where technically possible.

Ransomware & Corruption

Help restoring encrypted or corrupted files, databases and virtual machines.

Our approach

Stop. Don't try one more fix.

Repeatedly restarting a failing drive, running free recovery software or opening the device yourself can permanently destroy data that was still recoverable.

Power the device off and bring it to us. We diagnose first, tell you honestly what can be recovered and what it will cost, and only then proceed.
